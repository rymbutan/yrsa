FROM THE JOURNAL

Field notes, not just frames.

The stories behind the shot log — where Yrsa was standing, what the weather was doing, and what almost didn't make the frame.

04‑16 · SOUTHERN ARIZONA · 300MM

Desert nights run cold

Nobody warns you how fast the desert gives up its heat once the sun drops. By the time the sky went from oxide to ink, I was shooting in two jackets and wishing for a third.

Patience is the whole job out here — waiting for the wind to drop, the dust to settle, the last strip of light to catch the ridge line. Three hours for four frames. Worth it for the one that logged clean.

04‑16 · BISBEE, AZ · 90MM

Bisbee's backroads

An old copper town built into a canyon, with backroads that switch from pavement to gravel without warning. Borrowed a truck with no AC and drove them anyway.

The rust and oxide tones that show up across this whole archive started here — mining-town color, baked into every wall and fender in town.

07‑18 · CABOT TRAIL, NS · 35MM

Fog on the Cabot Trail

Three days on the highlands, and the fog only broke twice. Learned to keep the camera dry-bagged and ready rather than packed away — the gap never lasts more than a few minutes.

Green cliff, grey sea, one clean frame. Everything else in the log from that trip is fog.
