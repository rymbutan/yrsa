ABOUT

Yrsa shoots the in-between hours.

Working between the Sonoran desert and the Atlantic coast, Yrsa keeps a running shot log of wild places and the weather that passes over them — bone-dry afternoons, overcast lavender skies, and the last oxide light before dark.

Every image is logged with its date, place, and lens, treating the archive less like a portfolio and more like a field journal.
